MapIT Source Register - Job Roles and Certifications
MapIT Source Register
Sources used for IT role categories, job role mapping, and certification recommendations
Prepared for: Akshay Kumar | Date: 8 June 2026
Purpose: This PDF documents the official and reference sources used to build the earlier MapIT-style job role taxonomy and certification recommendation matrix. It is designed as a research-
source sheet for a career pathway or mind-map website, not as a final legal/accreditation guide. Certification names, costs, exam codes, and validity can change, so MapIT should periodically 
refresh source data from the official provider pages.
1. How to read this source register
• Role/category sources are workforce frameworks, occupation taxonomies, and official labor-market classifications. These help define role families such as IT Support, Cloud, Cybersecurity, 
Software Engineering, Data, ITSM, ERP/CRM, and leadership roles.
• Certification sources are primarily official vendor, standards-body, or government/industry skilling pages. These support recommendations such as CompTIA A+, ITIL, CCNA, AWS, Azure, 
Google Cloud, CISSP, ServiceNow, Salesforce, NIELIT, NSDC, FutureSkills Prime, and C-DAC.
• The earlier role list was synthesized from multiple taxonomies and market conventions. Some job titles are common industry titles and may not appear exactly in a single official taxonomy.
• For a production MapIT database, treat these sources as seed sources and add live job-posting research from LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Wellfound, company career pages, and ATS postings.

6. Recommended MapIT database fields for source-backed certification records
Field Description
Certification Name Official credential name from provider page.
Provider CompTIA, Microsoft, AWS, Google, Cisco, NIELIT, etc.
Official URL Canonical source page for verification and updates.
Role Families MapIT role categories connected to the credential.
Mapped Job Titles Specific job titles such as IT Support Analyst, Cloud Engineer, SOC Analyst.
Level Beginner, Associate, Intermediate, Professional, Advanced, Expert, Leadership.
Type Vendor-neutral, vendor-specific, national, academic, platform, framework.
Region Global, India, US, EU, or platform-specific.
Prerequisites Experience, training, exam prerequisites, or recommended background.
Skills Covered Skills and tools validated by the certification.
Exam / Assessment Exam code, project, performance-based exam, course completion, or badge.
Validity / Renewal Validity period and renewal mechanism where applicable.
Cost Range Public estimate or provider region-specific price, refreshed periodically.
Best For Role-readiness use case.
Not Best For Where the certification is weak or not necessary.
Next Certifications Recommended next step in the career ladder.
Market Signal How often it appears in job postings or whether it is optional/preferred/required.

7. Refresh and validation rules for MapIT
• Use official provider pages as the primary source for certification names, exam codes, retirement dates, validity, pricing and prerequisites.
• Use role/occupation taxonomies such as O*NET, BLS, SFIA, NICE, ESCO and NSDC as taxonomy sources, but validate current hiring demand through recent job postings.
• Separate three labels in the app: Required, Preferred, and Helpful. A certification should not be marked Required unless job postings frequently state it as mandatory for that specific role.
• For India pages, keep a separate India-recognized filter using NIELIT, NSDC/QP-NOS, FutureSkills Prime, C-DAC, SWAYAM/NPTEL and sector skill councils.
• Update cloud, AI, cybersecurity and vendor platform certifications at least every quarter because exam codes, names and versions change often.
• Do not use unofficial blogs as authoritative sources for exam names. Blogs can be used only for exam-prep context, salary context, or market commentary.
